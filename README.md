Cybersecurity Awareness Campaigns 

Welcome to my Cybersecurity Awareness Campaigns repository.

This repository documents my learning, research, and awareness activities across traditional cybersecurity and Web3 security.

The goal is to simplify cybersecurity concepts, share practical security lessons, and encourage safer digital habits.

 Campaigns

1. 31 Days of Cybersecurity Awareness

Platform: LinkedIn

A daily awareness campaign covering important cybersecurity topics, including:

- Phishing and social engineering
- Password and account security
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Malware and ransomware
- Data privacy and protection
- Email and device security
- Artificial Intelligence and cybersecurity
- Incident response

2. 31 Days of Web3 Security

Platform: X (Twitter)

A learning and awareness campaign exploring security challenges within the Web3 ecosystem.

Topics include:

- Wallet security
- Seed phrases and private keys
- Phishing and wallet drainers
- Malicious smart contract interactions
- Fake airdrops and crypto scams
- DeFi security
- Blockchain security awareness

 Purpose

- Promote cybersecurity awareness.
- Share knowledge in simple and understandable language.
- Document my continuous learning journey.
- Encourage safer practices in traditional technology and Web3.
- Connect with other learners and cybersecurity professionals.

 About This Repository

This is a personal learning and awareness project. The content is intended for educational purposes and does not replace professional security advice.

I welcome constructive feedback, corrections, and additional learning resources.
